## 001-hero-day-night-wood-swatches
* **Idea:** Luxury furniture hero section with Day and Night lighting toggle and zoomable wood texture swatches
* **Date:** 2026-11-05
* **Target Project:** FurniNest (Angular 21 + .NET 10 luxury furniture platform)
* **Status:** Wrapped Up (Approved)
* **Winner:** **Variant B** — Chosen for its intuitive before-and-after lighting split slider and high-impact 4K macro timber inspection modal.
* **Wrap-Up Spec:** [WRAP-UP.md](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/001-hero-day-night-wood-swatches/WRAP-UP.md)
* **Project Skill:** [.agents/skills/furninest-hero-split-slider/SKILL.md](file:///d:/boost/.agents/skills/furninest-hero-split-slider/SKILL.md)

### Variants
1. **[Variant A: Cinematic Loupe](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/001-hero-day-night-wood-swatches/variant-a.html)**
   * Full-bleed hero with 3 discrete lighting presets (Daylight 6500K, Golden Sunset 3200K, Midnight Lamp 2200K).
   * Night mode introduces an animated ambient warm lamp glow.
   * Right-side glassmorphic dock featuring an interactive magnifying loupe that tracks cursor movement across American Black Walnut, White Oak, and Malabar Teak.
2. **[Variant B: Split Wipe Slider](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/001-hero-day-night-wood-swatches/variant-b.html)**
   * Interactive draggable divider dividing 12:00 PM Natural Daylight and 10:30 PM Hearth Glow.
   * Floating hero card with price and Danish heritage copy.
   * Bottom horizontal material strip with a full 4K macro timber anatomy inspection modal.
3. **[Variant C: Kelvin Studio](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/001-hero-day-night-wood-swatches/variant-c.html)**
   * Two-column design atelier with the visual stage on the left and a lighting workbench on the right.
   * Continuous Kelvin color temperature slider (2200K – 6500K) dynamically updating the canvas tint and shadow contrast.
   * Wood species selector that dynamically recalculates the total piece price (e.g., +₹1,000 for Solid Oak, +₹2,500 for Heritage Teak).

## 003-mobile-filter-and-product-card
* **Idea:** Design an improved mobile filter bar and product card layout for FurniNest
* **Date:** 2026-11-05
* **Target Project:** FurniNest (Angular 21 + .NET 10 luxury furniture platform)
* **Status:** Wrapped Up (Approved)
* **Winner:** **Variant C** — Chosen for its floating glassmorphic bottom dock (optimal one-handed thumb ergonomics) and interactive timber swatches (live finish photo switcher).
* **Wrap-Up Spec:** [WRAP-UP.md](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/003-mobile-filter-and-product-card/WRAP-UP.md)
* **Index Hub:** [index.html](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/003-mobile-filter-and-product-card/index.html)

### Variants
1. **[Variant A: Sticky Pill Carousel & Minimalist Cards](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/003-mobile-filter-and-product-card/variant-a.html)**
   * Single-line horizontal swipeable chip carousels for Spaces and Budgets.
   * Eliminates multi-line wrapping on mobile screens completely.
   * 2-column responsive layout with 1-col / 2-col toggle switch.
   * Card timber swatches and instant `+ Add to Bag` action.
2. **[Variant B: Bottom Sheet Drawer & Editorial Story Cards](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/003-mobile-filter-and-product-card/variant-b.html)**
   * Ultra-compact filter anchor bar freeing 95% of screen height for pieces.
   * "Filter & Sort" trigger button reveals an iOS-style bottom sheet drawer with investment price slider and hardwood species selectors.
   * Full-width editorial cards with dimensions tag (`📐 W: 82cm • D: 78cm • H: 86cm`), craft badge, and dual CTA.
3. **[Variant C: Floating Glass Dock & Swatch Switcher](file:///d:/boost/.planning/sketches/003-mobile-filter-and-product-card/variant-c.html)**
   * Bottom floating glassmorphic dock optimized for one-handed thumb ergonomics.
   * Interactive timber swatch dots directly swap image finish/tint on tap (Walnut / Oak / Ebony).
   * Live scarcity urgency tags (`⚡ Only 2 in Stock`) and inline space popup drawer.
