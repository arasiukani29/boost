Sketch 003 — FurniNest Mobile Experience

Mobile Filter Bar & Product Card Redesign

Exploring 3 distinct architectural patterns to solve multi-row pill clutter, optimize phone ergonomics, and elevate FurniNest’s Scandinavian luxury aesthetic.

Variant A

Sticky Pill Carousel + 2-Col Minimalist

Replaces wrapping multi-line buttons with horizontal touch-scroll tracks (Categories + Budgets). Paired with clean 2-column cards and quick layout toggle.

  • ✓ Zero vertical screen clutter (single-line swipe)
  • ✓ 1-Col vs 2-Col live layout switcher
  • ✓ Timber swatches preview on card
  • ✓ One-tap "+ Add to Bag" action

Variant B

Bottom Sheet Drawer + Editorial Story

A single compact filter summary bar with a "Filter & Sort" trigger opening an iOS-style bottom sheet drawer with price sliders and hardwood species chips.

  • ✓ Maximum vertical space for product viewing
  • ✓ Continuous price investment slider
  • ✓ Full-width editorial cards with dimensions pill
  • ✓ High-end magazine feel with craft tags

Variant C

Floating Glass Dock + Swatch Switcher

A floating glassmorphic control dock pinned to bottom thumb reach. Cards feature interactive timber dots that switch photo finish tints dynamically.

  • ✓ Perfect one-handed thumb ergonomics
  • ✓ Dynamic timber finish preview (Walnut/Oak/Ebony)
  • ✓ Live stock urgency badges ("Only 2 left")
